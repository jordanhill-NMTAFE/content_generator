### Marking Guide: AT1 Example Assessment - AI Technology Evaluation

---

#### Task 1: Workplace Context Analysis
##### Instructions: 
Select and analyze a workplace context where AI technologies could provide value. Students must demonstrate understanding of organizational operations, technology landscape, and business objectives.

**Assessment Requirements:**
- Detailed organizational description (300-400 words)
- Current technology challenges identification
- Business goals and productivity objectives
- Stakeholder roles and responsibilities mapping
- Regulatory or compliance considerations

```
Example Response:
TechCorp Solutions is a mid-sized software development company with 150 employees specializing in custom business applications. Currently, the company faces challenges with manual code review processes that take 2-3 days per application release.

Current technology landscape includes Git version control, Jenkins CI/CD, and manual testing procedures. The primary business goal is to reduce development cycle time by 40% while maintaining code quality standards.

Key stakeholders include Development Team Lead (Sarah Johnson), Quality Assurance Manager (Mike Chen), and CTO (Dr. Alex Rivera). Compliance requirements include ISO 27001 for information security and GDPR for data protection.
```
---
*Students should demonstrate clear understanding of organizational context, identify genuine technology challenges, and show awareness of business objectives and stakeholder needs. Look for realistic scenarios and appropriate level of detail.*

---

#### Task 2: AI Technology Research and Identification
##### Instructions: 
Research and identify AI, ML, and DL technologies that address workplace challenges. Students must analyze at least three different technology approaches with technical understanding.

**Assessment Requirements:**
- Comprehensive technology research summary
- Analysis of AI vs ML vs DL approaches
- Technology capability mapping to business needs
- Technical requirements assessment
- Vendor or platform options analysis

```
Example Response:
**Artificial Intelligence Solution:**
Automated Code Review System using static analysis AI that can identify code patterns, security vulnerabilities, and compliance issues. Tools like DeepCode or SonarQube's AI features can analyze code quality automatically.

**Machine Learning Solution:**
Predictive Testing Framework using ML algorithms to predict which code changes are most likely to introduce bugs based on historical data patterns. This could prioritize testing efforts and reduce manual testing time.

**Deep Learning Solution:**
Natural Language Processing for Requirements Analysis using deep learning models to automatically analyze user requirements and suggest implementation approaches or identify potential conflicts.
```
---
*Students should demonstrate clear distinction between AI, ML, and DL technologies, show understanding of technical capabilities, and provide realistic analysis of how each technology addresses identified challenges.*

---

#### Task 3: Stakeholder Consultation and Requirements Confirmation
##### Instructions: 
Conduct consultation with relevant stakeholders to confirm requirements and validate technology analysis. Students must document consultation process and integrate feedback.

**Assessment Requirements:**
- Stakeholder identification and role analysis
- Consultation planning and methodology
- Documentation of consultation sessions
- Requirements validation matrix
- Integration of stakeholder feedback

```
Example Response:
**Stakeholder Consultation Plan:**

*Development Team Lead Consultation:*
- Meeting Date: Week 2, Day 3
- Focus: Technical feasibility and integration requirements
- Key Questions: Current tools compatibility, team training needs, implementation timeline

*QA Manager Consultation:*
- Meeting Date: Week 2, Day 5
- Focus: Quality standards and testing impact
- Key Questions: Quality metrics, testing process changes, compliance requirements

*CTO Consultation:*
- Meeting Date: Week 3, Day 1
- Focus: Strategic alignment and budget considerations
- Key Questions: ROI expectations, resource allocation, long-term technology strategy

**Requirements Validation Matrix:**
- Automated code review: Confirmed priority - High
- 24/7 availability: Confirmed requirement
- Integration with existing tools: Critical requirement
- Training budget: $10,000 allocated
```
---
*Students should demonstrate professional consultation approach, document interactions appropriately, and show evidence of stakeholder engagement. Look for realistic consultation methods and appropriate follow-up.*

---

#### Task 4: Implementation Proposal and Feasibility Analysis
##### Instructions: 
Develop comprehensive implementation proposal addressing technical, business, and organizational considerations. Include risk assessment and success metrics.

**Assessment Requirements:**
- Technology solution recommendation with justification
- Implementation timeline and resource requirements
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Success metrics and evaluation criteria
- Change management considerations

```
Example Response:
**Recommended Solution: AI-Powered Code Review System**

*Justification:*
Based on stakeholder consultation and technical analysis, an AI-powered code review system provides the best balance of immediate impact, technical feasibility, and cost-effectiveness. This solution addresses the primary challenge of review bottlenecks while maintaining quality standards.

*Implementation Timeline:*
- Phase 1: Tool selection and procurement (2 weeks)
- Phase 2: Integration and configuration (4 weeks)
- Phase 3: Team training and pilot testing (3 weeks)
- Phase 4: Full deployment and optimization (2 weeks)

*Risk Assessment:*
- Technical Risk: Integration complexity with existing tools (Medium risk - Mitigation: Pilot testing with limited scope)
- Organizational Risk: Team resistance to automated tools (Low risk - Mitigation: Comprehensive training and gradual rollout)
- Financial Risk: Cost overruns during implementation (Medium risk - Mitigation: Phased approach with budget checkpoints)

*Success Metrics:*
- Code review time reduction: Target 60% decrease
- Defect detection rate: Maintain current 95% standard
- Team productivity: 25% increase in deployment frequency
- User satisfaction: 80% positive feedback in 3-month survey
```
---
*Students should provide realistic and well-justified recommendations, demonstrate understanding of implementation challenges, and include appropriate risk management strategies.*

---

#### Task 5: Professional Presentation
##### Instructions: 
Deliver professional presentation summarizing findings and recommendations. Assessment focuses on communication skills, content organization, and stakeholder engagement.

**Assessment Requirements:**
- Clear presentation structure and logical flow
- Appropriate use of visual aids and technology
- Professional delivery and communication style
- Effective response to questions and feedback
- Demonstration of subject matter expertise

**Presentation Evaluation Criteria:**
- **Content Quality (40%)**: Accuracy, completeness, relevance
- **Organization (20%)**: Logical structure, clear flow, time management
- **Delivery (25%)**: Professional presentation, engagement, confidence
- **Visual Aids (10%)**: Appropriate use, clear graphics, readability
- **Q&A Handling (5%)**: Response quality, confidence, expertise demonstration

---
*Observe presentation delivery in real-time. Students should demonstrate professional communication skills, technical understanding, and ability to engage with audience questions.*

---

### Observation Checklist

#### Checkpoint:
- Student identified realistic workplace context with genuine technology challenges
- Student conducted appropriate research into AI, ML, and DL technologies
- Student demonstrated understanding of technology differences and capabilities
- Student planned and executed stakeholder consultation activities
- Student documented consultation outcomes and integrated feedback
- Student developed comprehensive implementation proposal with risk assessment
- Student presented findings professionally with appropriate visual aids
- Student responded effectively to questions and demonstrated subject expertise
- Student submitted all required evidence within specified timeframes
- Student demonstrated understanding of ethical and compliance considerations

---

#### Marking Criteria:
Each task should be evaluated based on the following scales:
- **Satisfactory (S)**: Student demonstrates competency and meets all requirements for the task
- **Not Yet Satisfactory (NYS)**: Student requires further development in one or more areas

**Specific Assessment Criteria:**

**Task 1 - Workplace Context Analysis:**
- **S**: Clear organizational description, realistic challenges identified, appropriate stakeholder mapping
- **NYS**: Vague or unrealistic context, missing key elements, inadequate stakeholder analysis

**Task 2 - Technology Research:**
- **S**: Demonstrates understanding of AI/ML/DL differences, appropriate technology selection, thorough research
- **NYS**: Confusion between technology types, inappropriate selections, superficial research

**Task 3 - Stakeholder Consultation:**
- **S**: Professional consultation approach, documented evidence, integrated feedback
- **NYS**: Inadequate consultation, poor documentation, failure to integrate feedback

**Task 4 - Implementation Proposal:**
- **S**: Realistic proposal, comprehensive risk assessment, appropriate timeline and resources
- **NYS**: Unrealistic proposal, inadequate risk analysis, missing critical elements

**Task 5 - Professional Presentation:**
- **S**: Professional delivery, clear communication, effective Q&A responses, appropriate visual aids
- **NYS**: Poor delivery, unclear communication, inadequate responses, poor visual aids

### Additional Feedback

**Strengths to Recognize:**
- Innovative technology solutions that demonstrate creative thinking
- Exceptional stakeholder engagement and professional consultation approach
- Comprehensive risk assessment with practical mitigation strategies
- Outstanding presentation skills and professional communication
- Strong integration of technical knowledge with business understanding

**Areas for Development:**
- **Technical Understanding**: Encourage deeper research into technology capabilities and limitations
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Provide guidance on professional consultation techniques
- **Risk Assessment**: Support development of systematic risk analysis skills
- **Communication Skills**: Offer specific feedback on presentation delivery and visual aid design
- **Critical Thinking**: Encourage evaluation of multiple solutions and trade-off analysis

**Learning Enhancement Suggestions:**
- Recommend additional research sources for emerging AI technologies
- Suggest professional development opportunities in stakeholder management
- Provide templates for systematic risk assessment and project planning
- Offer presentation skills workshops for continued improvement
- Connect with industry professionals for real-world perspective

**Recognition of Excellence:**
- Acknowledge students who demonstrate innovation in solution design
- Recognize exceptional research quality and comprehensive analysis
- Celebrate outstanding stakeholder engagement and professionalism
- Highlight effective integration of technical and business considerations
