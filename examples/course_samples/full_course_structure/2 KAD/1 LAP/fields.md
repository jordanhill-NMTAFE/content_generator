# Course Overview

This course provides students with comprehensive skills in artificial intelligence implementation within team environments. Students will develop both technical competencies and collaborative skills essential for modern IT workplaces.

## Key Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Team Collaboration**: Work effectively in diverse technology teams
2. **AI Assessment**: Identify and evaluate AI technology opportunities
3. **Implementation Planning**: Develop practical AI implementation strategies
4. **Ethical Considerations**: Apply ethical frameworks to AI deployment
5. **Communication**: Present technical concepts to various stakeholders

## Industry Relevance

The course directly addresses current industry needs for professionals who can:
- Bridge technical AI capabilities with business requirements
- Work collaboratively on complex technology projects
- Implement AI solutions responsibly and effectively
- Communicate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders

## Assessment Strategy

Assessments are designed to mirror real workplace scenarios, emphasizing:
- Practical application of AI technologies
- Team-based project work
- Professional communication skills
- Ethical decision-making in technology implementation
