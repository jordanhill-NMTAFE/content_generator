# Assessment Instructions

## Assessment Overview
This assessment requires you to identify and evaluate AI technologies suitable for implementation in a workplace context. You will work individually to research technologies, consult with stakeholders, and develop an implementation proposal.

### Duration: 3 weeks
### Submission Format: Written report (2000-2500 words) + 10-minute presentation

### Instructions:
1. **Select a workplace context** (current workplace, case study, or hypothetical organization)
2. **Research AI technologies** relevant to the selected context
3. **Consult with stakeholders** (simulated or actual) about requirements and constraints
4. **Develop implementation proposal** with feasibility analysis
5. **Present findings** to class demonstrating communication skills

### Submission Evidence:
Your submission must include:
- **Written Report**: Comprehensive analysis and proposal (2000-2500 words)
- **Stakeholder Consultation Log**: Evidence of consultation activities
- **Technology Comparison Matrix**: Structured analysis of options
- **Implementation Timeline**: Detailed project plan
- **Presentation Materials**: Slides and supporting materials
- **Reflection Statement**: Personal learning reflection (500 words)

# Assessment Instrument

## AT1 Example Assessment - AI Technology Evaluation

### Task 1: Workplace Context Analysis
#### Instructions:
Select and analyze a workplace context where AI technologies could provide value. This could be your current workplace, a case study provided, or a hypothetical organization of your choice.

Your response must include:
- Detailed description of the organization and its operations
- Current technology landscape and challenges
- Business goals and productivity objectives
- Stakeholder roles and responsibilities
- Regulatory or compliance considerations

**Evidence Required:**
- Organizational overview document (300-400 words)
- Current state analysis diagram
- Stakeholder mapping chart

Please provide your response here:

---

### Task 2: AI Technology Research and Identification
#### Instructions:
Research and identify AI, ML, and DL technologies that could address the workplace challenges identified in Task 1. You must identify at least three different technology approaches.

Your response must include:
- Comprehensive research of available technologies
- Analysis of AI vs ML vs DL approaches
- Technology capability mapping to business needs
- Technical requirements assessment
- Vendor or platform options analysis

**Evidence Required:**
- Technology research summary table
- Detailed analysis of each technology option (500-600 words each)
- Technical requirements specification
- Cost-benefit analysis framework

Please provide your response here:

---

### Task 3: Stakeholder Consultation and Requirements Confirmation
#### Instructions:
Conduct consultation with relevant stakeholders to confirm requirements, gather input, and validate your technology analysis. Document all consultation activities.

Your response must include:
- Stakeholder identification and role analysis
- Consultation planning and methodology
- Documentation of consultation sessions
- Requirements confirmation and validation
- Stakeholder feedback integration

**Evidence Required:**
- Stakeholder consultation plan
- Meeting notes or interview transcripts
- Requirements validation matrix
- Stakeholder feedback summary
- Revised requirements document

Please provide your response here:

---

### Task 4: Implementation Proposal and Feasibility Analysis
#### Instructions:
Develop a comprehensive implementation proposal that addresses technical, business, and organizational considerations. Include risk assessment and mitigation strategies.

Your response must include:
- Recommended technology solution with justification
- Implementation timeline and resource requirements
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Success metrics and evaluation criteria
- Change management considerations

**Evidence Required:**
- Executive summary (200 words)
- Detailed implementation plan (800-1000 words)
- Risk register with mitigation strategies
- Project timeline (Gantt chart or equivalent)
- Budget estimate and resource plan

Please provide your response here:

---

### Task 5: Professional Presentation
#### Instructions:
Prepare and deliver a professional presentation summarizing your findings and recommendations. The presentation should demonstrate effective communication skills and stakeholder engagement.

Your response must include:
- Clear presentation structure and flow
- Appropriate use of visual aids and technology
- Professional delivery and communication style
- Effective response to questions and feedback
- Demonstration of subject matter expertise

**Evidence Required:**
- Presentation slides (8-12 slides)
- Speaker notes or script outline
- Visual aids and supporting materials
- Peer feedback forms
- Self-reflection on presentation performance

**Presentation Guidelines:**
- Duration: 10 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Q&A
- Audience: Class peers and assessor
- Format: Formal business presentation
- Technology: PowerPoint or equivalent required

Please provide your response here:
