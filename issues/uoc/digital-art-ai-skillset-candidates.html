Digital Art + AI Skillset - Unit Candidates

Research conducted: 2026-01-30 Packages searched: CUA, ICT, UEE Focus: Units supporting digital art creation using AI techniques and AI as a subject for art

Overview

This skillset addresses the emerging intersection of artificial intelligence and creative arts practice. It covers:

  • Using AI tools and techniques to create digital art
  • Understanding AI/ML fundamentals for creative applications
  • Creating interactive and generative artworks
  • Exploring AI as both tool and subject matter in artistic practice

User-Identified Units

CUADIG519 — Investigate technologies for the creation of digital art

Attribute Value
AQF Level Diploma
Sector Visual Communication – Digital Content and Imaging
Package CUA (Creative Arts and Culture)
Qualifications CUA50720/25 Diploma of Graphic Design, CUA50920/25 Diploma of Photography and Digital Imaging, CUA51120/25 Diploma of Visual Arts, CUA60320/25 Advanced Diploma of Graphic Design, CUA60720/25 Advanced Diploma of Visual Arts

Why it fits: Core unit for investigating emerging digital art technologies including AI tools. Covers:

  • Evaluating current, emerging and experimental digital technologies
  • Researching capabilities of different technologies to achieve different effects
  • Experimenting with technologies to challenge and extend their capabilities
  • Establishing systems to maintain currency of knowledge

Performance Evidence: Investigate at least two different technologies for the creation of digital art.

UEEAS0006 — Use lead-free soldering techniques

Attribute Value
AQF Level Certificate III equivalent
Sector Electrotechnology
Package UEE (Electrotechnology)

Why it fits: Supports physical/electronic art installations and interactive works:

  • Essential for creating electronic art components
  • Enables building custom sensors, LED installations, interactive sculptures
  • Foundation skill for combining digital and physical art forms

Performance Evidence: Perform high reliability soldering/de-soldering, check components against specifications.

CUAWRT413 — Write poetry

Attribute Value
AQF Level Certificate IV
Sector Communication - Writing
Package CUA (Creative Arts and Culture)

  • Understanding creative writing forms, techniques, and literary devices

Attribute Value
AQF Level Diploma
Sector Internet of Things (IoT)
Package ICT (Information and Communications Technology)

ICT Artificial Intelligence Units

ICTAII401 — Identify opportunities to apply artificial intelligence, machine learning and deep learning

Attribute Value
AQF Level Certificate IV
Sector Artificial Intelligence
Package ICT (Information and Communications Technology)
Qualifications ICT40120 Certificate IV in Information Technology, ICT50220 Diploma of Information Technology

Why it fits: Foundation unit for understanding AI/ML/DL applications:

  • Identify opportunities where AI, ML and DL can be applied
  • Understand functions and features of industry-recognised AI technologies
  • Document advantages, disadvantages and implementation risks
  • Develop recommendations for AI implementation

Performance Evidence: Report on at least one opportunity for AI, ML, and DL technologies that may be applied in a workplace.

ICTAII501 — Automate work tasks using machine learning

Attribute Value
AQF Level Diploma
Sector Artificial Intelligence
Package ICT (Information and Communications Technology)

Why it fits: Practical ML model development applicable to generative art:

  • Understanding ML principles and techniques
  • Working with training datasets (structured, unstructured, labelled, unlabelled)
  • Understanding algorithms (regression, clustering, neural networks)
  • Creating and configuring ML models
  • Covers Australia's Artificial Intelligence Ethics Framework

Performance Evidence: Develop at least one ML model; use an algorithm to produce variable outputs on at least two occasions.

ICTAII502 — Train and evaluate machine learning models

Attribute Value
AQF Level Diploma
Sector Artificial Intelligence
Package ICT (Information and Communications Technology)

Why it fits: Advanced ML training for custom creative AI applications:

  • Training using supervised and unsupervised ML techniques
  • Working with training, validation and test data
  • Feature engineering and learning algorithms
  • Evaluating predictive accuracy of ML models

Performance Evidence: Train at least one ML model; evaluate the operations of at least one trained ML model.

Notes

  • CUA units are from the Creative Arts and Culture Training Package
  • ICT units are from the Information and Communications Technology Training Package
  • UEE units are from the Electrotechnology Training Package
  • ICTAII units are newly created (ICT Training Package Version 8.0) to address AI/ML skills needs
  • The ICTAII501 unit explicitly references Australia's Artificial Intelligence Ethics Framework
  • Cross-package imports may be required when building a skillset combining CUA and ICT units
  • Consider IP and copyright requirements when using AI tools (addressed in multiple CUA units)
