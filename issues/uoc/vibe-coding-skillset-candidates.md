# Vibe Coding Skillset - Unit Candidates

> Research conducted: 2026-01-30
> Packages searched: ICT, BSB, CUA
> Focus: Units emphasising researching and applying emerging technology trends

## Overview

"Vibe coding" is an emerging trend in software development where users leverage AI-powered tools to build software without traditional coding knowledge. These units would support a skillset for students learning to research, evaluate, and apply such emerging technologies.

---

## Primary Candidates (Highly Relevant)

### ICTICT426 — Identify and evaluate emerging technologies and practices
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **AQF Level** | Certificate IV |
| **Sector** | General ICT |
| **Qualifications** | ICT40118, ICT40120 (Certificate IV in Information Technology) |

**Why it fits:** This is the best fit for vibe coding. It explicitly covers:
- Identifying emerging technologies and practices in ICT
- Evaluating potential impact on organisational practices
- Developing strategies to respond to emerging tech (opportunities, threats, required changes)

**Performance Evidence:** Describe and evaluate 3 emerging technologies and 3 emerging practices, plus develop response strategies.

---

## Suggested Skillset Structure

A "Vibe Coding" or "AI-Assisted Development" skillset could be structured as:

### Option A: Certificate IV Level (3-4 units)
| Unit | Focus |
|------|-------|
| ICTICT426 | Research & evaluate emerging tech |
| ICTICT427 | Apply industry-specific technologies |
| BSBTEC403 | Integrate digital solutions (optional BSB crossover) |

|------|-------|
| ICTICT426 | Research & evaluate emerging tech |
