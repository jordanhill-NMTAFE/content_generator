# Digital Art + AI Skillset - Unit Candidates

> Research conducted: 2026-01-30
> Packages searched: CUA, ICT, UEE
> Focus: Units supporting digital art creation using AI techniques and AI as a subject for art

## Overview

This skillset addresses the emerging intersection of artificial intelligence and creative arts practice. It covers:
- Using AI tools and techniques to create digital art
- Understanding AI/ML fundamentals for creative applications
- Creating interactive and generative artworks
- Exploring AI as both tool and subject matter in artistic practice

## Notes

- **CUA units** are from the Creative Arts and Culture Training Package
- **ICT units** are from the Information and Communications Technology Training Package
- **UEE units** are from the Electrotechnology Training Package
- **ICTAII units** are newly created (ICT Training Package Version 8.0) to address AI/ML skills needs
- The ICTAII501 unit explicitly references **Australia's Artificial Intelligence Ethics Framework**
- Cross-package imports may be required when building a skillset combining CUA and ICT units
- Consider IP and copyright requirements when using AI tools (addressed in multiple CUA units)
